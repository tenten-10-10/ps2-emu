# Commercial disclosure activation gate

This file is an internal checklist, not a completed legal disclosure. Do not
publish it with placeholders and do not enable payment links until the owner has
reviewed the final page.

For a Japan-based operator, confirm with the selected payment provider and a
qualified adviser whether a Japanese Act on Specified Commercial Transactions
disclosure is required. At minimum, prepare and verify:

- Legal operator or business name
- Responsible representative, when applicable
- Physical business address and the legally permitted disclosure method
- Telephone/contact method and support email
- Price/amount selection and currency behavior
- Processor and any additional transaction or currency-conversion fees
- Payment timing and method
- What the payment does and does not provide
- Cancellation, mistaken-payment, duplicate-payment, fraud, and refund process
- Service availability and system requirements, if the payment is treated as
  consideration for digital content or a service
- Applicable tax wording

Do not describe the payment as a charitable donation. Do not promise a tax
deduction. Do not use a friends-and-family or personal-transfer flow for a
business support payment.
